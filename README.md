# 🏨 Mystery Hotel Booking Platform

### Affordable Luxury Stays with a Surprise Experience

A full-stack hotel booking platform that connects hotels with unsold room inventory to travellers looking for affordable luxury stays.

The platform allows customers to book premium hotel experiences at discounted prices without knowing the hotel's identity until the booking is confirmed.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Luxury hotels often have unsold rooms, especially during weekdays and off-peak seasons. At the same time, travellers are looking for premium accommodation at affordable prices.

The **Mystery Hotel Booking Platform** solves this problem by allowing hotels to sell vacant rooms at discounted rates while maintaining their regular pricing strategy.

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Location-Based Search:** Find mystery hotel deals by destination.
- **Hotel Category Selection:** Choose preferred hotel categories (4★ / 5★).
- **Amenity Filtering:** Filter hotels based on swimming pools, Wi-Fi, breakfast and other facilities.
- **Mystery Deals:** Discover premium hotels at discounted prices.
- **Secure Booking:** Book mystery hotel deals through an integrated payment system.
- **Hotel Identity Reveal:** Hotel name and address are revealed after successful booking.
- **Hotel Management:** Allow hotels to manage room inventory and discounted offers.
- **Booking Management:** Track reservations and payment status.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Select your travel destination.
2. Choose your preferred hotel category.
3. View available amenities and facilities.
4. Explore mystery hotel deals and discounted prices.
5. Complete the booking and payment.
6. Discover the actual hotel name and address after booking.

## 💡 Business Model

The platform follows a three-sided business model:

- **Hotels:** List unsold rooms at discounted rates and generate additional revenue.
- **Customers:** Enjoy luxury hotel stays at affordable prices.
- **Platform:** Earn commission on successful bookings.
